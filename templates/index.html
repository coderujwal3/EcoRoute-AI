Responsible-use notes

mandatory internship section
Environmental values are proxy estimates, not measured trip emissions. Car cost is configurable. Walking/cycling use zero operational trip cost and zero operational tailpipe emissions in this simplified model. The bundled GTFS feed is a demonstration dataset and should not be used for real-world navigation. AI explanations are grounded in structured decision results and are not permitted to invent transport facts.
